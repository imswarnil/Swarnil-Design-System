How to take it

Four weeks, in this order

You can binge it in an evening. The people who finish something afterwards do not.

  1. Week 1

    Watch, do not take notes

    The first pass is watching. Notes on a first pass are a transcription exercise, and transcription is not learning.

  2. Week 2

    Rebuild it without the video

    Same dataset, blank screen. This is the week that hurts and the week that works.

  3. Week 3

    Break it on purpose

    Wrong join, wrong grain, wrong filter. You will meet all three in production; better to meet them here.

  4. Week 4

    Ship something small

    One real dashboard for one real person. The project files include the brief I use.
