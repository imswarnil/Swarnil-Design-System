How it works

What you get, and what you do not

Yours to keep

Buy once, watch forever, download the project files. No subscription that quietly renews.

Questions answered

Every lesson has a thread. I read all of them and answer most, slowly, usually on a Sunday.

No certificate

There is no exam and no badge. What you get is a thing you built, which is the only credential that has ever helped me.

Do I need the gear you use?
No. Every course is filmed to be followed on a phone and a laptop. The gear pages exist because people ask, not because the courses need it.
Are the courses updated?
When the thing they teach changes, yes, and you get the update for free. When it does not, they stay as they are — a re-record for the sake of a new intro helps nobody.
Refunds?
Thirty days, no questions, one email. I have never asked anyone why and I am not about to start.
