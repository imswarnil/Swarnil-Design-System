Tools

What makes these videos

Every episode on this page was shot, lit, recorded and cut with exactly this. Nothing is sponsored.

  • Camera

    Sony FX3

    Full frame, no fan noise, and it does not overheat halfway through a take.

  • Lens

    Sigma 24-70 f/2.8

    One lens for the whole channel. Carrying four primes up a mountain is a bigger compromise.

  • Audio

    Shure SM7B

    Forgiving of a bad room, which is what every room I have filmed in has been.

  • Light

    Aputure 120d II

    One key light, bounced. Everything else in frame is the room doing its job.

  • Recorder

    Zoom F3

    32-bit float, so a take is never lost to a level I set wrong at 6am.

  • Edit

    DaVinci Resolve

    The grade and the cut are the same conversation.

The setup

One room, one light

The whole channel is filmed in the corner of a bedroom with a bedsheet on the window. The camera is expensive; nothing else is.
